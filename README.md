# MTK Private Business Manager

Install the app on your phone: https://mthokobless96-dev.github.io/mtk-private-business-manager/

On Android, open the link in Chrome and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Open it once while online before using it offline.

The app stores business records on this device and encrypts them with your app password. Forgotten passwords cannot recover encrypted records. JSON backup files are not encrypted; keep them private.
